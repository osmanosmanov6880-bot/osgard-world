# CITY REIGN — source-truth AAA BLOCKBUSTER MASTER ROADMAP

**Project:** CITY REIGN — ВЛАСТЬ НАД ГОРОДОМ. One original third-person/open-city game mixing driving, city management and saved consequences.

**Status:** playable Godot/WebGL engineering prototype, independent AAA commercial production **NOT APPROVED**. The word “AAA” here means the TARGET quality bar, not a delivered photoreal blockbuster. R90 is an unpublished staging line, protected from any master/public R87/SSH auto-release.

## Production principle
One authoritative `GameState` drives vehicle ownership, story mission progression, persistent 4-district city upgrades, transport, citizen reaction and economics. Screenshots, interface flourishes and version numbers do not count as player fun, device performance or commercial success. Every implementation must pass both native Godot logic checks AND rendered WebGL Chromium where relevant, followed by independent human gameplay/art review. No fabricated FPS, paid users, real assets, or false “100% AAA”.
