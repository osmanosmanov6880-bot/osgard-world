# OSGARD HORIZON DRIVE — Visual Milestone

This is an original Godot 4.4.1 racing/city visual upgrade. It does not contain any Forza Horizon 5 art or code.

Changes:
- Additional PBR sports coupe: slanted windshield and rear glass, black trim, alloy wheels, branded paint colors, high/low lighting surfaces, roof spoiler, animated rolling wheels.
- GPU-instanced road scenery: 810 crosswalk stripes, road reflectors, deterministic 192 decorative trees, 162 lamps, distant original mountain ring, festival gate.
- No gameplay collisions added to scenic set: player, traffic and missions remain functional and native tested.
- A 4.4.1 native test verifies the model, wheel pivots, instancing and road geometry.
- Materials are designed for Godot GL Compatibility HTML5. Premium look is the art direction, not a claim of Forza-grade photorealism.

Remaining before top-tier graphics: licensed/original high-resolution real-world car assets, road PBR textures, vegetation LODs, terrain sculpting and streaming, animation, particle effects, weather and time-of-day LUT, visual regression on browser GPUs, target platform profiling.
