# VECTOR//ZERO — sponsor pilot one-pager (draft)

## The offer

**A branded skill challenge inside OSGARD PLAY.** Sponsor one clearly-labelled daily field contract in VECTOR//ZERO: the brand is attached to a real player objective, an optional CTA and a shareable result card. The audience is invited to beat a time, not to click through a forced full-screen ad.

No audience size, impression guarantee or revenue claim is made until the relevant production metrics exist.

## Pilot packages — pricing hypotheses to validate

| Package | Test quote | Deliverables |
|---|---:|---|
| **Launch Partner / 7 days** | **$500–$1,000** | Sponsor label on one daily contract, one branded CTA, short campaign report from available consented/attributable metrics |
| **Field Season / 30 days** | **$2,500–$5,000** | Branded rule/contract for a month, CTA, result-card co-branding, weekly aggregate report if data volume supports it |
| **OSGARD Multi-Game / 30 days** | **$7,500–$15,000** | Multi-game sponsorship across selected OSGARD titles, campaign creative and consolidated aggregate report — only once each placement is live and measured |

These are **initial quote hypotheses**, not a verified market price list. Final scope and price depend on actual available traffic, creative effort, partner fit and measurement quality. For an early pilot, offer a smaller fixed-fee design/validation package if there is not yet enough audience data to justify a larger quote.

## What a sponsor receives

- Clearly-labelled placement; no disguised advertising.
- One unique CTA/Telegram start payload to separate enquiries from other sources.
- A dated campaign code and a written definition of each metric.
- An end-of-campaign report with observed clicks/leads/contracts, not estimated “reach” presented as fact.
- No exclusive rights, guaranteed installs, revenue-share promises or audience guarantees unless separately agreed in writing.

## Measurement and privacy

Client-side events are queued only when the player has explicitly accepted the OSGARD game analytics consent and has a valid login token. A player may still play, save a local record and share the challenge without allowing analytics. The sponsor link uses the Telegram bot start parameter `vector_zero_sponsor`; actual qualified enquiries must be counted in the bot/CRM, not inferred from button impressions. The result card is a client-side artifact, not a server-verified score.

The report should separate: campaign page visits (if collected), CTA clicks, Telegram starts, qualified conversations, signed agreements and paid invoices. A click is not a lead, and a lead is not revenue.

## 30-day sales test

1. Prepare one sample branded card and a sponsor mock-up labelled “concept / not a live campaign”.
2. Contact 5–10 relevant brands directly with a personalised short message.
3. Record replies, qualified calls, written proposals, contracts and paid invoices.
4. If no qualified conversation appears, change the segment and offer before building ad delivery or payment infrastructure.
5. Do not publish fabricated player counts, stars, sponsor logos or “sold out” claims.

Предлагаем протестировать маркированное спонсорское испытание VECTOR//ZERO на 7 дней: фирменная подача задания, переход к вашему предложению и краткий отчёт по фактически доступным метрикам. Не обещаем охваты, которых пока не измерили; начнём с пилота с фиксированным объёмом работ. Могу прислать одностраничный пример интеграции и обсудить, подходит ли он вашему бренду?

## Success condition

The pilot is worth expanding only if it produces verified qualified enquiries or a repeat purchase at an acceptable delivery cost. If the report only shows clicks and no relevant conversations, do not scale the offer unchanged.
