THE BRONZE HOUR

A bronze clock has thirteen hands, though only three cast shadows.

Find the hour the mechanism forgot.

01 · Gear

A tooth clicks into place without touching another.

02 · Shadow

A fourth shadow points beneath the dial.

03 · Bell

A low note rings once, though no hammer falls.
