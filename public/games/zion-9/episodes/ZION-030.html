Category · climateHealth and access

The pain is not “summer.” It is unequal access to cool space.

Sun-exposed pavement can become much hotter than shaded ground. Older people, outdoor workers, children, pregnant people and people with chronic illness are among the groups the city warns are more vulnerable during heatwaves.

ELSTAT's 2021 census records 643,452 residents for the Municipality of Athens; CNI Greece repeats the same census figure. This is population context only — not an estimate of heat casualties and not a claim that all residents are equally exposed.

ELSTAT · 2021 Census ↗ · CNI Greece · population cross-check ↗ · City heat-safety service ↗

Evidence rule: 60°C and 30°C are surface-temperature readings reported for distinct exposure conditions, not air-temperature measurements. The −5°C and 70% rainwater figures for Cooling Havens are programme targets described by the municipality, not independently verified outcomes for every site.

02 / Technology already in use

NOT SCIENCE FICTION
Operational public information

#CoolAthens + heat-risk intelligence

The Municipality of Athens says its heatwave categorisation was built using two decades of historical weather and mortality data, in collaboration with the National Observatory of Athens and other partners. The municipal portal links to temperature and thermal-discomfort maps updated every 15 minutes and describes the EXTREMA app’s heat-risk, cool-route and nearby-cooling-place features.

Open official #CoolAthens service ↗

Interventions + monitoring

Cooling Havens and shade-first design

The Athens programme specifies rainwater collection and reuse, rain gardens, natural filtration channels, blue-green public spaces and IoT sensors. Its public programme page describes planned interventions across multiple neighbourhoods and co-design with residents. Target figures on that page are not presented here as independently verified outcomes.

Separately, the EU city initiative reports measured results from 2025 interventions: tree shade at Argentinis Dimokratias Square, cool materials and shade at Agia Irini, a microforest at Ano Kypseli and a cooling garden at Serafio.
