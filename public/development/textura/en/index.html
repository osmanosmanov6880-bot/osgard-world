01 / DIGITAL ART DIRECTION

Luxury is not louder.
It is more deliberate.

TEXTURA’s visual language relies on restraint rather than decoration: espresso shadows, warm paper, a quiet metallic note and generous negative space. The product remains the focus — coffee, objects and place.

01

Editorial by design

Serif-led typography, concise copy and clear hierarchy instead of a catalogue that competes for attention.

02

Product with texture

Menu and objects are framed as curated selections, each with context and a clear next step.

03

Calm interaction

Selection, quantities, totals and empty states stay legible on smaller screens.

02 / PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

From atmosphere
to a clear choice.

The storefront establishes a route: philosophy, signature menu, space, journal, objects and answers to common questions. The selection basket previews chosen items and an estimated total; it does not pretend to be a live checkout.

04

Maintain

A local-only Content Studio supports content editing plus JSON import/export.

TEXTURA — luxury specialty coffee digital experience by OSGARD Development. Responsive editorial storefront; signature menu; local selection basket; JSON-backed content; local-only Content Studio; security headers and a tested static release workflow. Demo prices and venue details; no live checkout or payment integration.

03 / ENGINEERING

Polished on the surface.Measured underneath.
  • ✳Responsive interface. Desktop and mobile layouts, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.
  • ✳Content separated from UI. Site content is maintained through structured JSON.
  • ✳Local-only Content Studio. Import/export workflow; intentionally excluded from the public build.
  • ✳Security-aware release. CSP and HTTP security-header configuration, local audits and release checks.
  • ✳No fabricated outcomes. No invented conversion metrics, reviews or production claims.
